## 1. LLM-as-judge — vieses medidos, júri e rúbrica

- Zheng et al., *Judging LLM-as-a-Judge (MT-Bench)* — https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05685 · https://arxiv.org/html/2306.05685
  - "strong LLM judges like GPT-4 can match both controlled and crowdsourced human preferences well, achieving over 80% agreement"
  - limitações: "position, verbosity, and self-enhancement biases, as well as limited reasoning ability"
  - "Only GPT-4 outputs consistent results in more than 60% of cases" (ao inverter a ordem); "Position bias can be very significant"
  - "GPT-4 favors itself with a 10% higher win rate; Claude-v1 favors itself with a 25% higher win rate"
- Panickssery et al., *self-preference / self-recognition* — https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.13076
  - "self-preference, where an LLM evaluator scores its own outputs higher than others' while human annotators consider them of equal quality"
  - "a linear correlation between self-recognition capability and the strength of self-preference bias"
- Verga et al., *PoLL — Panel of LLM evaluators* — https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.18796 · https://arxiv.org/html/2404.18796
  - "using a PoLL composed of a larger number of smaller models outperforms a single large judge"
  - "exhibits less intra-model bias due to its composition of disjoint model families"
  - "Running the entire three model PoLL is seven to eight times less expensive than running a single GPT-4 judge"
  - "the highest positive delta for each individual model being scored occurs when it is judged by itself"
- *Judging the Judges* — https://arxiv.org/html/2406.12624
  - "the best scoring judge, Llama-3 70B, is 8 points behind human judgment"; humanos entre si: "Scott's π of 96.2±1.07"
  - "even judges that have more than 90% may still differ more than 10 points in their assigned score"
- PReMISE — https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30803
  - "changing the rubric changes the response quality measurement induced by a fixed judge"
  - "A vague rubric asking for a response to be `helpful and factual` can reward polished answers that invent facts"
  - refinar a rúbrica "reduces the rate at which exploit responses receive high scores from 46.4% to 36.0%"
- Rúbrica fixa por questão — https://arxiv.org/html/2512.16041
  - rúbrica explícita gerada uma vez e aplicada fixa: "reducing IPI and TOV scores by 16.1% and 11.0%"
- Evidently AI, *LLM-as-a-judge guide* — https://www.evidentlyai.com/llm-guide/llm-as-a-judge
  - "Binary evaluations, like 'Polite' vs. 'Impolite,' tend to be more reliable"
  - "If you have several aspects to evaluate … it's best to split them into separate evaluators"
  - "Non-technical team members — like product or subject matter experts — play a big role in setting the evaluations"

## 2. Anthropic — critérios, rúbrica, código antes de modelo

- *Develop tests* — https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/test-and-evaluate/develop-tests
  - "Have detailed, clear rubrics: 'The answer should always mention 'Acme Inc.' in the first sentence. If it does not, the answer is automatically graded as 'incorrect.''"
  - "Empirical or specific: … instruct the LLM to output only 'correct' or 'incorrect' … Purely qualitative evaluations are hard to assess quickly and at scale"
  - "Encourage reasoning: Ask the LLM to reason first before producing an evaluation score"
  - "Generally best practice to use a different model to evaluate than the model used to generate the evaluated output"
  - "choose the fastest, most reliable, most scalable method: 1. Code-based grading: Fastest and most reliable, extremely scalable … 3. LLM-based grading: … Test to ensure reliability first then scale."
- *Define success* — https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/test-and-evaluate/define-success
  - "Specific: … Instead of 'good performance,' specify 'accurate sentiment classification.'"; "Measurable: Use quantitative metrics or well-defined qualitative scales"
- *Demystifying evals for AI agents* — https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/demystifying-evals-for-ai-agents
  - code-based: "Fast, Cheap, Objective, Reproducible, Easy to debug"; model-based: "Non-deterministic, More expensive than code"
  - "You won't know if your graders are working well unless you read the transcripts and grades from many trials"
- *Building effective agents* — https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents
  - evaluator-optimizer "is particularly effective when we have clear evaluation criteria, and when iterative refinement provides measurable value"
  - "finding the simplest solution possible, and only increasing complexity when needed"

## 4. Claude Code — hooks em `-p`, `--settings`, timeout, `$N`

- *Headless* — https://code.claude.com/docs/en/headless
  - "Without `--bare`, a `-p` session runs the hooks in a project's `.claude/settings.json` and connects the servers in its `.mcp.json`, even in a folder you've never trusted."
  - "Add `--bare` to reduce startup time by skipping auto-discovery of hooks, skills, custom commands…"
  - "Claude Code exits with code 143 … records no result for it" e "runs no hook other than `SessionEnd`" (SIGTERM)
  - "User-invoked skills and custom commands work in `-p` mode: include `/skill-name` in the prompt string"
- *CLI reference* — https://code.claude.com/docs/en/cli-reference
  - `--settings`: "Path to a settings JSON file or an inline JSON string. Values you set here override the same keys in your `settings.json` files for this session."
  - `--max-turns`: "Limit the number of agentic turns (print mode only). Exits with an error when the limit is reached. No limit by default."
- *Hooks* — https://code.claude.com/docs/en/hooks
  - "Hook entries merge across settings levels rather than replacing each other"
  - Stop: `"decision": "block", "reason"`: "Must be provided when Claude is blocked from stopping."; "`Stop` | Yes | Prevents Claude from stopping, continues the conversation"
  - "The blocking message is the reason from your JSON's blocking decision when it makes one, and your stderr text otherwise."
  - "Claude Code registers them when you or Claude invoke the skill, including in a `-p` run in a folder you haven't trusted." (hooks de skill)
  - `pass --settings '{"disableAllHooks": true}'`, "which takes precedence over project and local settings"
- *Hooks guide* — https://code.claude.com/docs/en/hooks-guide
  - "`Stop` and `SubagentStop`: the `reason` is fed back to Claude so it keeps working, unless the response also sets `"impossible": true`"
- *Errors* — https://code.claude.com/docs/en/errors
  - `API_TIMEOUT_MS`: "Per-request timeout in milliseconds" (padrão 600000)
- *Agent SDK — agent loop / python* — https://code.claude.com/docs/en/agent-sdk/agent-loop · https://code.claude.com/docs/en/agent-sdk/python
  - "`error_max_turns` | Hit the `maxTurns` limit before finishing | No"; "The underlying Claude Code process also exits with a nonzero code."
  - `is_error` "always True on the error_* subtypes, False on success"; `num_turns`, `session_id`, `total_cost_usd` sempre presentes
- *Skills* — https://code.claude.com/docs/en/skills
  - "Arguments are available via the `$ARGUMENTS` placeholder."; "To access individual arguments by position, use `$ARGUMENTS[N]` or the shorter `$N`"
  - "An indexed placeholder with no corresponding argument … stays in the content unchanged."
  - "To include a literal `$` before a digit, `ARGUMENTS`, or a declared argument name, such as `$1.00` in prose, escape it with a backslash: `\$1.00`. A backslash before any other `$` is left unchanged."
  - `disable-model-invocation`: "Set to `true` to prevent Claude from automatically loading this skill. Use for workflows you want to trigger manually with `/name`."
  - `user-invocable`: "Set to `false` when only Claude should invoke the skill"
  - `allowed-tools`: "Tools Claude can use without asking permission during the turn that invokes this skill. The grant clears when you send your next message."
- *Slash commands* — https://code.claude.com/docs/en/slash-commands
  - "Custom commands have been merged into skills. … `.claude/commands/deploy.md` and … `.claude/skills/deploy/SKILL.md` both create `/deploy`"
