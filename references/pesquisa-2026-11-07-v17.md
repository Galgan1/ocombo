## F1 — Anthropic, "How we built our multi-agent research system"

https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/multi-agent-research-system

- "Scale effort to query complexity. Agents struggle to judge appropriate effort for different tasks, so we embedded
  scaling rules in the prompts. Simple fact-finding requires just 1 agent with 3-10 tool calls, direct comparisons might
  need 2-4 subagents with 10-15 calls each, and complex research might use more than 10 subagents with clearly divided
  responsibilities."
- "These explicit guidelines help the lead agent allocate resources efficiently and prevent overinvestment in simple
  queries, which was a common failure mode in our early versions."

Leitura: o defeito de 07/11 é conhecido e tem remédio publicado: regra de escala ESCRITA, com faixas, no texto que o
agente líder lê antes de agir. Daí o Passo 0 no topo do SKILL.md e os limiares em `scripts/porta.py`.
